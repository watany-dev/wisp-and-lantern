# Playtest protocol

## Session rules

- At most 15 minutes. A parent sits next to the child. Do not explain the game.
- If the child is stuck, wait 30 seconds, then give one hint.
- After playing, ask three questions (in Japanese, recorded verbatim in hiragana):
  1. "What did the ghost do?"
  2. "Why did it bring the wrong one?"
  3. "What do you do before you take it?"
- Records are **anonymous verbatim quotes**. Expressions and behavior go in a separate field.
- Audio recording only for the maintainer's own child, never published. Other children: handwritten notes only. No photos or video.
- Friends' children: record the parent's verbal consent (`consent: verbal yes, <date>`). Names are A/B/C.
- 2026-11-14: show the trial 4 artwork (shadows, key) before play and record the reaction.
- 2026-11-07 (#1): record audio; if `docs/evidence/origin.md` is still empty, write it from this session.
